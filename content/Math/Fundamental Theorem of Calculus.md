
# Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
Let $f(x)$ be a function of one variable. Then the fundamental theorem of calculus states that:

$$
\int_{a}^{b} \frac{d f(x)}{dx} dx = f(b) - f(a) 
$$
We can also write this as

$$
\int_{a}^{b} F(x) dx = f(b) - f(a) 
$$
When we set $\frac{df}{dx} = F(x)$. To integrate a function $F$, just think up a function $f(x)$ whose derivative is $F(x)$.
