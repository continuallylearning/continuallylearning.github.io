# Divergence theorem (Fundamental Theorems for Divergences)
Let $\vec{F}$ be a vector field. Then the fundamental theorem for divergences / divergence theorem states that:

$$
\oint_{S} \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{a} = \int_V (\nabla \cdot \vec{F}) d \tau
$$
Where $S$ is a closed surface that encloses a volume $V$. It is implied that $\vec{a}$ is a vector that is normal and outwards from the surface $S$, and that $d\tau$ is a scalar representing the differentiable volume at some point inside the volume.
