# Gradient Theorem
The gradient theorem, also known as the fundamental theorem of calculus for line integrals, says that the line integral of the gradient of a scalar field, $\nabla f$, is equal to evaluating the difference of the original scalar field $f$ at the endpoints $b$ and $a$:

$$
\int_a^b (\nabla f) \cdot d\vec{l} = f(b) - f(a)
$$
